Overview: Earth Surrounded

Our planet is encircled by a dangerous cloud of space debris. Each orbital zone tells a story of human activity and its consequences.

🔴 LEO: The Critical Zone

Most Dangerous: Low Earth Orbit contains 80% of all debris. Satellites here face constant collision threats from fragments traveling at 17,500 mph.

🔵 MEO: Navigation Under Threat

GPS at Risk: Medium Earth Orbit hosts our navigation satellites. Debris here could cripple global positioning systems worldwide.

🟢 GEO: Communication Crisis

Billion-Dollar Targets: Geostationary orbit contains our most expensive satellites. One collision could cost billions and disrupt global communications.

The Kessler Syndrome

Cascading Disaster: One collision creates thousands of new fragments, triggering a chain reaction that could make space unusable for generations.
